ACKNOWLEDGEMENT
 
Acknowledgment is a practice in project writing to appreciate and give credence to all that made the project works a reality either directly or indirectly. In academics however, after thanking God if you believe in one, the next person is your supervisor, followed by academic and non academic staffs, your colleagues and other people you may want to appreciate.
